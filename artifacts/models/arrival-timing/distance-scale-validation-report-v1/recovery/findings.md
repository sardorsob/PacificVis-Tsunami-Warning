# Distance-dependent reported-duration spread: computational checks

45 of 45 predefined synthetic datasets completed in 45 attempts. The fixed NCEI exploration design has 1,064 records and 33 events. Actual reported responses did not enter these simulated likelihoods. All attempts, including failed precision checks, remain in the diagnostics table.

The six population parameters are alpha and beta (log-ratio center/distance adjustment), tau (between-event log-ratio SD), and three residual SD anchors at500/2000/5000km. SD1000 is the interpolation midpoint of the first two anchors. The first effect is the lexically first fitted event's latent effect, reconstructed by its exact conditional Normal. These quantities describe composite reports, not physical noise or accuracy.

Each setting allocates 15 independent datasets; completed counts are in the table. Coverage is the fraction of true values inside central posterior 90% intervals. Wilson 95% intervals express finite-replicate uncertainty; individual fluctuations do not certify or refute identifiability. Contraction is one minus posterior/prior central-90% width, where analytic prior widths exist. Standardized error uses posterior SD and is not a Normal-test z.

Recovery and calibration under an assumed simulator do not establish model adequacy, predictive transfer, archive representativeness or a model-selection decision. The 18 reserved outcomes remain sealed.
