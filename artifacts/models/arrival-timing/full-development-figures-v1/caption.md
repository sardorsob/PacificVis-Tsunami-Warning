Selected source-specific full-development models, using saved native-minute quantiles only. Dark bands show central 90% pointwise posterior intervals for the population median; light bands show central 90% pointwise predictive intervals for a new report. Dots mark saved population-median q50 values at nine distances; connectors and filled bands join those points and do not add evaluated predictions. These are not simultaneous bands. NCEI prediction includes a new event offset plus report variation; the selected T-OBS and T-CAL forms have no event term. NCEI and T-OBS use logarithmic duration axes; T-CAL uses signed minutes, where positive means the estimate is later than the observed report. All distance axes are logarithmic. Hatched regions lie outside the training distance range. NCEI residual spread is held at its endpoint value below 500 km and above 5,000 km, which is separate from distance extrapolation. These archive-conditional curves do not establish calibration, physical-onset accuracy, or operational warning performance.
