Quick descriptive profile of the final development corpus. Source waveform rows
were not reparsed a second time: all1768normalized files were freshly checksum-
verified against the manifest and prior independent raw-row conservation proof.
All1177arrival-report rows were recounted from compressed tables;342corpus files
were rehashed. Rates count retained source snapshots and may repeat measurements.

No universal missingness percentage is identifiable: missing returned values,
absent requested timestamps, unsupported sources and unresolved arrival semantics
have different denominators. Archive sentinel conventions remain unverified;
finite numeric values are not certified valid physical measurements.

MCAR is a poor general working assumption given provider/format/event selection.
MAR could be a conditional modeling assumption; MNAR is a plausible sensitivity
scenario for detectability-dependent arrival reporting. Neither is demonstrated.
See https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7615108/ (definitions and identifiability).
No formal MCAR test, imputation, model fit or new onset validation was performed.
